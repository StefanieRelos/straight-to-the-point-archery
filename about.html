Our Story

Founded in 2010, Straight to the Point Archery began with a simple mission. Located in Tacoma, we have grown into a premier facility featuring indoor ranges for year-round practice.

Our Facility

Our state-of-the-art indoor range is designed for archers of all levels. From professional timing systems to high-quality backstops, we ensure a premium experience.

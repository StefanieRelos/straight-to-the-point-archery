About Us

Straight to the Point Archery was founded with a simple mission: to provide high-quality archery instruction in a safe and welcoming environment.

Our Story

Located in the heart of Tacoma, we have been serving the community since 2010. Our facility features state-of-the-art equipment and indoor ranges that allow for year-round practice, regardless of the weather.

Our Philosophy

We believe that archery is for everyone. Whether you are 8 or 80, our certified instructors are here to help you develop your focus, patience, and precision through the ancient art of the bow.
